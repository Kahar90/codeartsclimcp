GLANCE CONTRACT (shared by the three glance lanes; do not change it)

Every plan object from GET /api/plans gets `glance: { state, word, detail, at }`:
- state: one of "working" | "needs-you" | "failed" | "done" | "quiet" | "idle"
- word: "Working" | "Needs you" | "Failed" | "Done" | "Check Claude" | "Idle"
- detail: one short line (under 90 chars), for example "2 of 3 lanes running, step 4/7",
  "Claude is asking: merge now or wait?", "rm-core failed: checks failing", "All 3 lanes finished",
  "No lead update for 18m; Claude may be waiting on you".
- at: ISO time the state was reached (best effort) or null.

Colors (both views): working cyan #5ee0f0, needs-you amber #ffb547, failed red #ff6b6b, done green #6ee7a0,
quiet amber #ffb547 (dimmer, no pulse), idle grey #9a93a8.
Glyphs (write them as JS unicode escapes): working U+25CF (filled dot), needs-you "!", failed U+2715 (x),
done U+2713 (check), quiet "?", idle U+2013 (dash).
Priority when combining several plans (worst first): needs-you, failed, quiet, working, done, idle.
Tab title: "<glyph> <word> - <plan title>" (idle: the view's plain title).
Favicon: a 32x32 canvas data: URL, rounded square in the state color with the glyph in dark #1a1424.
