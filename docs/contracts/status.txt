STATUS CONTRACT (shared by the status-truth, office-fit and lanes-truth lanes; do not change it)

Goal: a person glancing at either view must never see two statuses that disagree, and must be able to
tell from each agent alone whether it is working, finished fine, or needs them.

1. Lane status (server, runner/controls.mjs laneOutcome, carried as `lane.status` everywhere) keeps
   { key, label, tone, reason } and adds:
   - attention: boolean. True only when the human must act: failed, checks failing, unverified,
     needs review, paused, supervisor died, stalled (the same lanes the Needs you bucket holds). False for running,
     planned, done, verified, handled/merged, stopped, killed, dropped, continued, and for every ended lane of a
     closed plan (the lead closed it, so nothing is left for the human).
   - steps: "live" | "complete" | "partial" | "hide". Completion comes only from real step evidence
     ("step N" subjects), never from the result alone.
     live: the lane is running or planned; show "step d/t" (or "d/t steps").
     complete: every numbered step is done; show "t/t" with a check mark.
     partial: the lane has a known step list and some steps are still open (done, verified, merged,
     handled, stopped, failed, dropped, paused...); show the real "d/t" fraction and the open steps,
     never a forced "t/t".
     hide: no known step list (a legacy lane whose progress cannot be trusted, or an older payload):
     never show a step fraction on the label or badge; the lane details may still list the steps.
   - glyph: one of the glance glyphs: working U+25CF, needs-you "!", failed U+2715, done U+2713,
     idle U+2013. Running lanes "working"; attention lanes "!" (failed and checks failing use the x);
     done, verified, merged and handled use the check; dropped, continued and stopped-but-handled
     use the dash.
   Labels: handled reads "Handled by lead" (or "Merged" when the ack note says merged); stopped reads
   "Stopped by lead" when the lead stopped it.

2. Plan (server, planView): `plan.badge = { word, tone, glyph }` is exactly the glance word, tone and
   glyph (tone: working run, needs-you warn, failed bad, done done, quiet warn, idle idle). Every
   plan-level pill, popover heading and rail badge in both views shows plan.badge, never a word
   computed in the page. A closed plan's glance detail is the close reason without a leading
   "Closed:" or "Done:".

3. Views fall back to their current behaviour when a field is missing (older server).
